Hottext announces its minimum-choices status instead of only showing it (WCAG 4.1.3 Status Messages). The interaction joins the shared `MinimumRequirementHint` pattern every other min-choices interaction uses: while the selected hottexts are short of `minChoices`, the authored `data-min-selections-message` (or the localized "Select at least N") shows as the hint paragraph, the answer group names it in `aria-describedby`, and a response change that leaves the minimum unmet is announced politely once per unmet stretch — silent on further changes while it stays unmet, re-announced after the minimum is met and falls short again. Visible change: the footer's "✓ Minimum met" / "Select at least N" badges are gone — the requirement states itself as the shared hint while unmet and clears when met, as in choice, order, match, associate, hotspot and the rest, and the hint shows only while an enabled candidate can answer (the badges rendered in scorer and disabled views too). The "Selected: N / M" footer counter is unchanged, and the cataloged `interactions.hottext.minimumMet` key is now unreferenced (left in place for the i18n package's owners to retire).
