to-pie recognizes an interaction under a namespace prefix and in any case. A prefixed portable custom interaction or prefixed interaction beside a standard one refuses the item, where it used to convert the standard interaction alone and drop the other without warning. A lowercase-authored QTI 2.2 interaction converts through its handler, and custom-interaction evidence reads the PCI type identifier in its QTI 2.2 and QTI 3.0 spellings.
