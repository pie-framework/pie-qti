`@pie-qti/qti-common/accessibility` maps APIP 1.0 access elements to and from QTI 3 support cards: `readApipAccessElements` reads them with their content links, and `writeApipAccessibility` writes cards as `apip:apipAccessibility`, reporting the supports APIP has no element for.
