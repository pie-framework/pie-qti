The item session's built-in `completionStatus` now follows the QTI ASI §5.2.1 transitions table instead of jumping to its final value. **Breaking:** consumers reading `completionStatus` from the session view (`ItemSession.state()`, dispatch transitions, `ItemSessionActionResult.completionStatus`) see `unknown` from the moment the attempt has begun — the candidate's first response change (including a mounted PCI module reporting its own change and a `setResponses`/`openSession({ responses })` override), or activating a suspended handoff (`openSession({ restore, activate: true })`) — where they previously saw `not_attempted` until submission, and `completed` after a `scoreAttempt`-only flow where they previously stayed at `not_attempted`. `completed` remains the outcome of response processing completing a non-adaptive item's attempt (or an adaptive item's own rules); the engine never assigns `incomplete` itself, and `restoreResponses`/session restore keep the saved status instead of beginning an attempt. Items that already author `completionStatus` assignments in response processing keep governing their own value at those points.
